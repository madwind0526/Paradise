Day-sky pictures (jpg, png or webp). The app picks them up automatically.

- Each time the day starts (dusk for night, dawn for day) one of them is drawn at random. With no picture here, the built-in sky is used.
- A picture is shown as a window on the sky: its bottom edge is the horizon, its top edge is 55 degrees up, and it repeats mirrored sideways.
  Sky only: no ground, trees, mountains or sun.
- Tall pictures (9:16, for example 896x1600) work well.
- The built-in ones are anime skies made with Krea 2, based on the earlier photo-like set (see docs/ASSETS.md).
